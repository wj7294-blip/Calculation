## KQ1. What are the newest (2025–2026) SAM-at-anode concepts and which groups lead them?

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 anode-side SAM literature is dominated by (i) Li-metal/anode-free Cu current collectors moving from coin cells to multi-Ah pouch cells (Shandong Univ., Adv. Mater. 2026, 8–10 Ah anode-free pouches with an MTZ SAM on Cu), (ii) aqueous Zn anodes, now the most prolific SAM sub-field (thiol, silane, carboxylic-acid, amino-acid SAMs; crystal-plane-selective "shielding" SAMs for (002) texture), (iii) first SAM concepts for Mg (EDL regulation via a fluorinated silane, Angew. 2025) and for Li | quasi-solid/polymer interfaces (Si-monolayer "bridging" to in-situ PDOL, J. Power Sources 2025; SAM on LLZTO in hybrid electrolytes, Angew. 2025), and (iv) SAMs on graphite for low-temperature and fast-charge LIBs. No 2025–2026 SAM paper was found for Na, K, Ca or Al metal anodes.

## KQ2. Which SAM concepts exist for cathodes / Al foil but not anodes (and why)? And vice versa?

### Takeaway
Cathode-side SAM work (2016–2026) is smaller in volume but has three concepts with no anode analog: (1) vapor-phase/gas-phase SAM deposition on powders at 130–150 °C framed explicitly as "mass-scalable" (Ni-rich NCM, LNMO), (2) SAMs for processing/manufacturing robustness (ambient-air-stable Ni-rich powders, binder-adhesion promotion, moisture/solvent-tolerant sulfide catholytes), and (3) a quantitative dipole/"interfacial polarity" descriptor on active-material surfaces (Nat. Nanotechnol. 2026). Conversely, EDL/Helmholtz-plane engineering, anion-immobilizing charged SAMs, facet-selective texture control and polymer-electrolyte "bridging" SAMs exist only at anodes. No peer-reviewed SAM study on Al cathode current collectors in a battery was found at all.

### Inferences
- Cathode → anode transfer candidates (cathode precedent, no anode analog found): (a) gas-phase SAM deposition on powders/foils at 130–150 °C ("mass-scalable", 2018/2021) — no vapor-phase SAM on Cu foil or Li for batteries was found; (b) SAM-for-manufacturing (dry-room relaxation, binder adhesion, slurry compatibility) — at anodes only the 2018 Li–CNT slurry paper and the 2025 J. Power Sources air-stability remark touch this; (c) the Nat. Nanotechnol. 2026 "interfacial polarity" descriptor and terminal-group electronic-structure tuning — anode papers invoke lithiophilicity/zincophilicity qualitatively but no 2025–2026 anode paper was found that frames SAM design via a dipole/polarity descriptor or measures work-function shifts; (d) SAM on the solid electrolyte itself (LPSCl catholyte) — the anode-side equivalent (SAM on SE facing Li, or on Cu facing SE in anode-free ASSB) was not found.
- Anode → cathode transfer candidates (anode precedent, no cathode analog found): EDL/Helmholtz-plane regulation by a dilute SAM-forming additive (Mg, Zn), charged SAMs that immobilize anions and raise t+ (Li), facet-selective shielding SAMs for texture (Zn (002)), and covalently bridging SAMs to in-situ polymer electrolytes (Li | PDOL).
- Likely reasons for the Al-foil SAM void: (i) Al already passivates (native oxide → AlF3 in LiPF6), so the baseline problem is salt-specific (LiFSI/LiTFSI) rather than universal; (ii) a monolayer adds contact resistance at a surface whose only job is electronic contact; (iii) the industrial fix is carbon-coated Al foil, already commodity. These are inferences; no source states them directly.
- Why anode concepts have not moved to cathodes: EDL/charged-SAM effects target metal nucleation and anion depletion, which do not exist at intercalation cathodes; facet shielding is meaningful for plating metals, not for fixed oxide particles.

## KQ3. Are there SAM studies at solid-state anode interfaces (Li | SE, anode-free Cu | SE)?

### Takeaway
Only indirect or hybrid cases exist: a SAM on LLZTO inside a hybrid quasi-solid electrolyte (Angew. 2025), a Si-monolayer bridging Li to an in-situ PDOL polymer electrolyte (J. Power Sources 2025), a SAM on Li6PS5Cl used on the catholyte side (Adv. ## KQ5. What do 2025–2026 perspectives/roadmaps say about molecular monolayers in batteries?

### Takeaway
The main 2026 perspective (ACS Nano, SINANO/CAS) frames self-assembled molecular interfaces through four mechanisms — interfacial-energy regulation, passivation against reactive species, control of the local microenvironment, and interphase films acting as SEI/CEI precursors — and moves the agenda from passive "surface passivation" toward "programmable interphase engineering", explicitly raising molecular design, manufacturing compatibility and application-specific strategies. A 2026 Nature Nanotechnology Research Briefing elevates "interfacial polarity" to a design descriptor.

### Cited Findings
- Zhao, J.; Dong, Q.; Shao, H.; Shen, Y.; Chen, L.
