# Self-Assembled Monolayers (SAMs) and Molecular Monolayer Coatings on Zinc Metal Anodes (2018–2026): Survey and Gap Analysis

**Method / verification caveat.** In this session the publisher hosts (Wiley, ACS, Elsevier/ScienceDirect, Nature, Science, PubMed/PMC, ResearchGate, OSTI, Crossref API, Semantic Scholar API) could not be fetched (network allow-list), so every entry below was assembled from search-engine abstracts/snippets of the primary papers (searched 31 Oct 2026). Bibliographic identifiers (journal, volume, DOI) are given where the search result stated them explicitly; items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** are figures or attributions that came only from a secondary snippet (e.g., a citing paper's introduction) or that were internally inconsistent across sources. Metrics are as reported by the authors under their own test conditions and are not directly comparable across papers.

## Key Question 1: Which SAM / monolayer molecules on Zn have been published, and what metrics did they report?

### Takeaway
Between 2021 and 2026 roughly 15–20 primary papers report a true monolayer-scale organic film on a Zn (or Zn-supporting) anode, split into (a) ex situ chemisorbed SAMs — thiols (MUDA, aromatic/alkyl thiols, PFDT), silanes (fluoroalkylsilane, trifluoropropyl-trimethoxysilane, GPTMS, KH590/TFPS), carboxylic acids (dodecanoic acid, theanine), an octadecyl-phosphate complex layer, and a polystyrene brush — and (b) in situ "self-adsorbed/self-assembled" monolayers formed from electrolyte additives (1-cyclohexyl-2-pyrrolidone, BMIM⁺-on-MXene, HTFSI, "SA", "SAB", selective-shielding molecules). Almost all are in mild ZnSO₄ (sometimes Zn(OTf)₂) electrolytes on thick Zn foil, with headline metrics of Zn‖Cu CE 99.3–99.7 % and symmetric-cell life 1,400–4,300 h at 1–10 mA cm⁻² and ≤2.5 mAh cm⁻²; (002) texturing is claimed by thiol and "selective shielding" SAMs.

### Inferences
- The field has converged on a single narrative ("hydrophobic tail blocks water/HER + zincophilic head/anchor homogenizes Zn²⁺ flux + facet-selective adsorption → (002)"), reused across thiol, silane and carboxylate chemistries with near-identical metric envelopes (CE 99.3–99.7 %, 1,400–4,300 h symmetric). Differentiation between anchor chemistries is therefore weak in the current literature.
- The only ex situ SAM papers that approach practical conditions are eScience 2024 (90 % DOD, but only 180–200 h and on Cu) and ESM 2025 (N/P 2.11, 30 µL mAh⁻¹). Everything else uses thick Zn foil and flooded coin cells.
- "Phosphonic acid SAM on Zn" essentially does not exist as a distinct study; the single phosphorus-headed layer (OPA, J. Energy Chem. 2024) is described as an in situ Zn-complex film and its exact head group is ambiguous.

### Gaps
- Exact molecule identity and test conditions for the ACS Nano 2022 thiol paper; exact CE/cycle numbers for Small 2022 (MUDA), E&F 2025 (DA), CEJ 2025 (GPTMS), ACS AMI 2021 (PS brush) could not be extracted (publisher pages blocked).
- No alkanethiol chain-length series (C6–C18) on Zn was found; the only chain-length SAM/HER study is on alkaline iron electrodes (Manohar/Narayan, *JACS* 2012, DOI 10.1021/ja3095119) — [ACS](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ja3095119).
- No study of true ODPA or other phosphonic-acid SAMs on Zn anodes; no betaine/glycine or genuine zwitterionic SAM (zwitterions appear only as electrolyte co-solutes).

## Key Question 2: How do papers address the fundamental problem that the SAM sits on a surface that dissolves on stripping?

### Takeaway
No paper found explicitly quantifies monolayer loss/retention after repeated stripping; the field handles the problem by four implicit routes — (i) anchor the SAM on a non-stripping scaffold (Cu, Cu nanoclusters, MXene), (ii) use an electrolyte-dissolved molecule so the monolayer is a dynamic adsorption equilibrium that re-forms, (iii) claim Zn²⁺ permeates and plates *beneath* an electrically insulating monolayer that "floats" on the growing/receding surface, or (iv) assert "self-healing" without direct evidence. Post-cycling characterization is limited to morphology, impedance and ex situ XPS/FTIR at formation.

### Cited Findings
- **Route (i): SAM on a non-stripped substrate.** 4Mpy SAM is bound to Cu (Cu–S), and Zn is plated/stripped on top; this enabled 90 % DOD and an anode-free cell (Zhang et al., eScience 2024). — [SciEngine](https://www.sciengine.com/doi/10.1016/j.esci.2023.100205). FAS monolayer is chemically bonded to rough Cu nanoclusters on Zn, which the authors say keeps it attached under mechanical deformation, with Zn depositing "beneath the monolayer" (ESM 2025). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S240582972500279X). BMIM⁺ SAM is anchored on a Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene interlayer rather than on Zn itself (Chen et al., Angew. 2024). — [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/anie.202404825)
- **Route (ii): dynamic additive-formed monolayers.** CHP "self-adsorbed monolayer" is formed from the electrolyte and the cell "consistently maintains lower impedance during cycling" (Huo et al., Adv. Sci. 2025). — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11848536/). GPTMS hydrolyzes in the electrolyte and "spontaneously" self-assembles (CEJ 2025). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1385894725117208). Hyaluronate layer "rebuilds itself spontaneously and dynamically" via a capture–deposition mechanism (CEJ 2025). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1385894725044766). Fulvic acid forms an electric-field-responsive, self-adaptive EDL that restructures during cycling (ESM 2026). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2405829726003314)
- **Route (iii): plating beneath an insulating monolayer.** The FAS paper states the "exceptional electrical insulating property ensures uniform Zn deposition beneath the monolayer" (ESM 2025). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S240582972500279X). The PFDT paper reports post-cycling roughness lower than bare Zn and a 40th-cycle Zn‖Cu voltage gap of 37 mV vs 41 mV, but does not characterize the chemical state of the monolayer after cycling. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12824933)
- **Route (iv): asserted self-healing.** The PFDT paper's framing lists "self-healing characteristics" among generic SAM virtues (together with ion-selective transport, controllable hydrophobicity, mechanical flexibility) without a dedicated test. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12824933)
- **Stated stability claims without mechanism:** MUDA SAM "shows excellent stability during prolonged Zn plating/stripping within a wide range of pH" (Mwemezi et al., Small 2022). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35460179/)
- **Motivation given for chemisorbed SAMs:** physically deposited coatings "detach during cycling" because of poor adhesion, whereas chemisorbed SAMs are argued to adhere (PFDT paper introduction). — [ACS Omega](https://pubs.acs.org/acsodf/article/11/2/3569/5077631/PFDT-Self-Assembled-Monolayers-for-Stable-Zinc)
- **Characterization status:** The Adv. Sci. (CHP) paper used FTIR and XPS to confirm SAM *formation*, not post-cycling fate; a search for post-mortem XPS depth-profile/re-adsorption studies of SAMs on Zn returned nothing. — [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202413731)
- **Field-level recognition that ex situ inference is insufficient:** an arXiv operando-visualization study of anode-free high-utilization aqueous cells (Sn) argues that plating/stripping "cannot be reliably inferred from ex-situ measurements" (not a Zn-SAM study). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26727)

### Inferences
- The "dissolving substrate" paradox is largely side-stepped rather than solved: the most robust configurations move the anchor to Cu/MXene (which is, in effect, an anode-free or host-type architecture) or rely on an additive reservoir in the electrolyte. For a truly ex situ SAM on bare Zn foil, the claim of long life is likely to depend on (a) a thin cycled layer relative to foil thickness (low DOD), so most of the original SAM-bearing surface is never stripped, and (b) Zn plating under the layer; neither has been demonstrated directly.
- "In situ" monolayer additives blur into conventional electrolyte-additive literature; their advantage (self-renewal) comes at the cost of additive consumption, cathode crossover, and lean-electrolyte incompatibility — none of which were examined in the SAM-labelled papers found.

### Gaps
- No quantitative measurement of SAM surface coverage vs cycle number (e.g., XPS S 2p / F 1s or ToF-SIMS after 10/100/1,000 cycles), no electrolyte assay for desorbed thiol/silane, and no isotopically labelled monolayer tracking were found.
- No operando/in situ spectroscopy (SFG, ATR-FTIR, Raman, EQCM, in situ AFM) on a SAM-coated Zn anode during stripping/plating was found.
- No study tests an ex situ SAM at ≥50 % DOD on Zn foil (the only ≥50 % DOD result is on a Cu-anchored SAM).

## Key Question 3: Are there anode-free Zn (SAM on Cu/Ti/stainless steel) studies?

### Takeaway
Exactly one SAM-based anode-free Zn result was found (4-mercaptopyridine on Cu, eScience 2024: anode-free 4Mpy/Cu‖graphite, 150 cycles at 0.1 A g⁻¹); no SAM on Ti or stainless steel for Zn, and no SAM-on-Cu anode-free study with a high-capacity cathode (MnO₂, V₂O₅, I₂, Br₂) or lean electrolyte. Anode-free Zn work otherwise uses inorganic/carbon current-collector treatments.

### Cited Findings
- 4Mpy SAM on Cu: anode-free 4Mpy/Cu‖graphite cell 150 cycles at 0.1 A g⁻¹ with no obvious fading; Zn/4Mpy/Cu 90 % DOD for 180–200 h at 10 mA cm⁻²; MnO₂ pouch cell 100 cycles at 78.8 % Zn utilization (Zhang et al., eScience 2024). — [SciEngine](https://www.sciengine.com/doi/10.1016/j.esci.2023.100205)
- Non-SAM anode-free benchmarks: HCl-treated Cu foil with conformal Cl layer → modified-Cu‖ZnₓMnO₂ anode-free full cell 500 cycles at 80 % retention (Shen et al., *Adv. Funct. Mater.* 2025, DOI 10.1002/adfm.202504042). — [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adfm.202504042). Sb@Cu collector anode-free Zn–Br₂ cell, 1,500 mAh, ~13.9 mAh cm⁻² (*Nat. Commun.* 2022). — [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-35630-6). Carbon-nanodisc-coated Cu (KAUST/Stanford 2021). — [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2021/01/210120085052.htm)
- Review of high-utilization/anode-free Zn design strategies (Nano-Micro Lett. 2024, DOI 10.1007/s40820-023-01304-1) — does not list SAMs as an anode-free strategy per search snippet. — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40820-023-01304-1)
- Precedent from Li metal: 3-mercaptopropionic acid SAM on planar Cu gives 17.6 mV nucleation overpotential and 98.4 % CE over 250 cycles (*Electrochim. Acta* 2024); a patent on thiol-type monolayers on Cu hosts for anode-free Li cells exists. — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0013468624002421); [USPTO](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12658447)
- Ti appears as a Zn substrate only with a supramolecular/lipopeptide-type interphase (avg. CE 99.6 % at 1 mA cm⁻² / 1 mAh cm⁻², Sci. Adv. 2024 context), not a thiol/silane SAM **[UNVERIFIED linkage]**. — [Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn2265)
- No stainless-steel SAM result for Zn appeared in any search.

### Inferences
- SAM-on-Cu is a natural fit for anode-free Zn because the anchor survives full stripping; the single eScience 2024 result (short-chain aromatic thiol, graphite cathode, low rate) suggests the space is almost entirely open, including: alkyl vs aromatic thiol series on Cu, silane/phosphonate SAMs on Ti/SS (where thiols bind poorly but phosphonates/silanes bind oxides), and SAM + inorganic (Cl, Sb, Sn) hybrid collectors.

### Gaps
- No anode-free SAM study with cathode-prezincated chemistries (ZnₓMnO₂, ZnₓV₂O₅), lean electrolyte, or pouch format.
- No SAM study on Ti or stainless-steel current collectors for Zn.

## Key Question 4: Any SAM studies in alkaline Zn systems (Zn–air, Zn–Ni, Zn–MnO₂) or in non-aqueous Zn electrolytes?

### Takeaway
Alkaline: no ex situ SAM on Zn in KOH was found; the closest is a dithiol + phosphate-surfactant corrosion-inhibitor additive (99.9 % self-corrosion inhibition in concentrated KOH for Zn–air, JCIS 2024), plus the MUDA paper's untested claim of zincate resistance over a wide pH range. Non-aqueous: no SAM-on-Zn study in any organic Zn electrolyte (Zn(TFSI)₂/AN, PC, glyme, ionic liquid) was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Alkaline Zn–air, thiol additive:** Cen et al., "Synergistic effect of surfactant and 1,10-decanedithiol as corrosion inhibitor for zinc anode in alkaline electrolyte of zinc-air batteries", *J. Colloid Interface Sci.* 2024, 659. OP-10P (octylphenol polyoxyethylene ether phosphate) + 1,10-decanedithiol (DD) in concentrated KOH; 99.9 % inhibition efficiency of Zn self-corrosion during dormancy; a later paper states DD adsorbs preferentially via thiol–Zn coordination to form a hydrophobic film (surfactant named OP-10 vs OP-10P inconsistently). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38160645/); [citing paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0013468625015452)
- **Alkaline Zn, other molecular inhibitors (not SAMs):** DDBAB surfactant 98.38 % inhibition at 60 ppm in KOH (ionic-liquid additive for Zn–air, *Electrochim. Acta* 2024). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0013468624014439). Surfactant-type additive for alkaline Zn–MnO₂ (*Electrochim. Acta* 2025). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0013468625023618). PEI inhibits HER and Zn corrosion (review snippet). — [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1581000)
- **MUDA (Small 2022)** claims carboxylate groups repel OH⁻ and resist zincate formation "across a wide pH range", the only ex situ SAM paper gesturing toward alkaline relevance; test electrolyte appears to be mild (not confirmed). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35460179/)
- **Alkaline precedent on another metal:** n-alkanethiol SAMs on rechargeable iron electrodes in alkaline electrolyte suppress HER by >90 % (increasing with chain length; patent gives >90 % at n = 6) — Manohar/Narayan, *JACS* 2012, DOI 10.1021/ja3095119 — demonstrating that alkanethiol SAMs can survive and function in concentrated alkali. — [ACS](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ja3095119); [USPTO](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/9577298)
- **Zn–Ni:** only a Ni-coating/Zn–Ni alloy protective-layer patent surfaced; no SAM. — [USPTO](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4735876)
- **Alkaline Zn–air anode state of the art (non-SAM):** Sb-modified porous lamellar Zn in KOH–ZnO, 580.7 mAh g⁻¹, ~10× cycle life of bare Zn (2026). — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13159586)
- **Non-aqueous baseline electrolytes (no SAM):** AN–Zn(TFSI)₂, AN–Zn(OTf)₂, PC–Zn(TFSI)₂ give ≥99 % Zn CE and anodic stability to ~3.8 V (Han et al., *ACS AMI* 2015). — [LBL](https://perssongroup.lbl.gov/papers/acsami2015-nonaqzn.pdf). 1 M Zn(TFSI)₂/AN: ~28 mS cm⁻¹, >1,000 h Zn cycling (2020). — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/342855287_Acetonitrile-Based_Electrolytes_for_Rechargeable_Zinc_Batteries)
- Searches combining "self-assembled monolayer" with Zn(TFSI)₂/acetonitrile/non-aqueous zinc returned only aqueous SAM papers. — [search context](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.5c03770)

### Inferences
- Alkaline SAM work on Zn is a genuine white space: the iron-electrode precedent shows alkanethiol SAMs can suppress HER >90 % in KOH, and the JCIS 2024 dithiol additive shows thiol–Zn coordination survives concentrated alkali, yet nobody has reported an ex situ thiol/silane/phosphonate SAM on Zn in a Zn–air or Zn–Ni cell with shape-change/HER/CE metrics. Phosphonate and silane anchors (hydrolytically labile Si–O at high pH) would need alkali-stability screening; thiolates and possibly catechols are the more plausible candidates.
- Non-aqueous Zn + SAM is entirely unreported; since Zn CE is already ≥99 % in AN/Zn(TFSI)₂, the value proposition would be nucleation control/dendrite suppression and interphase chemistry rather than HER suppression.

### Gaps
- No ex situ SAM on Zn tested in KOH (Zn–air/Zn–Ni/alkaline Zn–MnO₂) — no data on alkali stability of Zn–S, Zn–O–P, Zn–O–Si bonds under cycling.
- No SAM on Zn in any non-aqueous Zn electrolyte.

## Key Question 5: What computational studies exist, and what do reviews list as open questions?

### Takeaway
Computation in SAM-on-Zn papers is almost exclusively static DFT (adsorption energies of H₂O/molecule on Zn facets, frontier orbitals, chain-length/head-group screening); no ab initio MD, classical MD of a SAM–Zn–water interface, or machine-learned interatomic potential (MLIP) study for a SAM on Zn was found. Reviews (JACS 2021 SAM perspective; Zn-anode and in situ-characterization reviews 2024–2025) flag SAM–substrate bond strength, interface stability, and the lack of operando methods as open issues, but no dedicated SAM-on-Zn review exists.

### Inferences

- Because no review focuses on SAMs on Zn, the field lacks a standardized comparison (coverage, tilt angle, contact angle, HER current at fixed potential, CE at fixed DOD), which inflates apparent novelty across chemically similar papers.

### Gaps
- No MD/AIMD/MLIP study of a SAM–Zn–water interface; no computation of the SAM's fate during Zn stripping.
- JACS 2021 perspective's specific list of challenges and its Zn content could not be confirmed (full text inaccessible).

## Key Question 6 (synthesis): Where is the literature saturated vs genuinely sparse?

### Takeaway
Saturated: single-component hydrophobic/zincophilic SAMs (thiol, silane, carboxylate) on thick Zn foil in 2 M ZnSO₄ with symmetric-cell hour counting and Zn‖Cu CE, plus a very large adjacent literature of in situ self-assembling additives. Sparse-to-empty: phosphonic-acid SAMs, alkanethiol chain-length series, mixed/patterned SAMs, dipole-engineered SAMs with measured work-function/(002) correlation, SAM fate under stripping, DOD ≥ 50 % on SAM-coated Zn foil, lean electrolyte/pouch data, anode-free SAM collectors beyond one paper, alkaline and non-aqueous SAMs, MD/MLIP, and operando spectroscopy.

### Cited Findings
- **Saturated zone evidence:** ≥8 primary papers (2022–2026) report thiol/silane/carboxylate SAMs on Zn foil with Zn‖Cu CE 99.3–99.7 % and symmetric life 1,400–4,300 h at 1–10 mA cm⁻² (see KQ1 entries: ACS Nano 2022; Small 2022; ESM 2025; ACS Omega 2026; Small 2026; ACS AMI 2025; Adv. Sci. 2025; E&F 2025). — e.g., [ACS Omega](https://pubs.acs.org/acsodf/article/11/2/3569/5077631/PFDT-Self-Assembled-Monolayers-for-Stable-Zinc); [Wiley Small 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smll.75025)
- **In situ self-assembling additive literature is large** (GPTMS, ethylene sulfate, BSA, 2-pyridinesulfate, phytic-acid-montmorillonite, polyanionic additives, hydrated eutectic bilayers…). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2405829725005422); [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11740509/); [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11434035/)
- **Only ≥50 % DOD SAM data point:** 4Mpy/Cu at 90 % DOD, 180–200 h (eScience 2024); only lean-electrolyte SAM data point: FAS-Cu@Zn at 30 µL mAh⁻¹, N/P 2.11 (ESM 2025) **[UNVERIFIED]**; only SAM pouch cell: CHP NVO‖Zn 200 cycles (Adv. Sci. 2025, additive-type). — [SciEngine](https://www.sciengine.com/doi/10.1016/j.esci.2023.100205); [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S240582972500279X); [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202413731)
- **Highest-areal-capacity SAM result:** S-SAM Zn‖V₂O₅ >11 mAh cm⁻², 128 cycles; cumulative plating >2.875 Ah cm⁻² (AFM 2026, in situ type). — [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/adfm.202532042)
- **Mixed/binary/patterned SAMs:** found only in molecular electronics (work-function tuning with mixed dipolar SAMs), none on Zn or any metal anode. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.11261); [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11538666/)
- **Phosphonic acid SAM background exists only on ZnO/Cu surfaces** (coverage/ordering vs chain length; contact angle 77–88°; C6 phosphonate protects ZnO from acid), not on Zn anodes. — [OUCI](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/en/works/988dO8q9/); [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S002197972030936X)

### Inferences (gap map for idea generation)
- **Molecule classes not yet tried on Zn anodes:** phosphonic acids (ODPA, fluorinated phosphonates, bis-phosphonates), hydroxamic acids, catechols/dopamine-type monolayers (alkali-stable candidates), N-heterocyclic carbenes, alkanethiol homologous series, true zwitterionic (sulfobetaine/phosphorylcholine) SAMs, mixed-length or mixed-head-group SAMs, photo-/micro-patterned SAMs to template deposition sites, dipole-reversed pairs (e.g., –CF₃ vs –NH₂ terminated) with Kelvin-probe work-function and (002) texture correlation.
- **Architectural gaps:** SAM on Ti/SS/Cu for anode-free Zn with prezincated cathodes; SAM on Cu-coated Zn or Zn-powder electrodes (one GPTMS powder result exists); SAM + eutectic/hybrid electrolytes; SAM in non-aqueous Zn(TFSI)₂/AN or glyme; SAM in KOH for Zn–air/Zn–Ni with shape-change and HER (EC-GC) metrics.
- **Measurement gaps:** monolayer coverage vs cycle number; desorbed-molecule assay in electrolyte; operando SFG/ATR-IR/EQCM during stripping; HER quantified by EC-GC or DEMS rather than LSV onset; CE at fixed DOD ≥ 50 % and ≤20 µL mAh⁻¹.
- **Computation gaps:** classical MD/MLIP of SAM packing, water penetration and Zn²⁺ permeation; free-energy of thiolate/phosphonate desorption during Zn oxidative dissolution; dipole-layer electrostatics vs facet nucleation energetics.

### Gaps
- Because publisher full texts were blocked, several "first/only" statements above are based on the absence of search hits rather than exhaustive database queries (Google Scholar/Scopus/Web of Science were not accessible); the report writer should treat "no study found" as "none surfaced in ~35 web searches on 31 Oct 2026", not as proof of absence.
- Chinese-language literature and ChemRxiv/arXiv 2025–2026 preprints were only incidentally covered (one arXiv Zn interphase preprint, 2609.03176, surfaced but is a conductive mesh interphase, not a SAM). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03176)
