## Key Question 1 — What grafting chemistries have been applied to Si anodes, and with what results?

### Takeaway
Five grafting families have been demonstrated on Si anodes: (a) alkoxysilane SAMs on the native SiOx shell (epoxy-, amino-, thiol/sulfonate-, vinyl-, fluoro-silanes), (b) hydrosilylation of H-terminated Si giving Si–C bonds (alkenes bearing alkyl, oligo-EO/epoxy, cyclic carbonate, pyridine, pyrrolidone, carboxylic acid), (c) aryl-diazonium grafting (4-carboxyphenyl; aryl bridges to graphite; ATRP initiators), (d) methyl/siloxane termination of Si nanowires and wafer-type model electrodes, and (e) SAM-initiated polymer layers (PMMA brushes, PAA, PANI, polyimide). Reported gains are mostly half-cell ICE increases of ~10–15 absolute points (e.g., 62.4→76.3 % for PMMA-tethered Si films; 89.8 % ICE for C–F-grafted SiNPs), reduced SEI impedance growth, and improved retention over 100–300 cycles in nano-Si half cells; only two works (SiO/SAM/polyimide; cyclic-carbonate-grafted SiNPs) report full-cell data, and none report commercially relevant loadings. Hydrophobic alkyl (C12) monolayers electronically isolate Si and kill capacity, while oligo-EO coatings isolate Si once thickness reaches the ~2.5 nm tunneling limit — an important design constraint. **Phosphonic acid SAMs and chlorosilane (e.g., OTS) SAMs on Si anodes were not found at all.**

### Inferences
- The field has converged on three functional "jobs" for a monolayer: (1) passivating the reactive Si–H/Si–OH surface (methyl, alkyl, PEO), (2) templating SEI chemistry (cyclic carbonate, C–F, sulfonate), and (3) acting as a covalent primer for binders/polymers (epoxy, amine, vinyl, ATRP initiators). Few papers try to do all three with one molecule.
- The NREL C12/PEO result implies that chemically "perfect" dense alkyl SAMs are counterproductive for a conversion/alloying anode: electronic tunneling through the SAM must remain possible (≤~2.5 nm), so monolayer design for Si differs fundamentally from SAM design for corrosion or electronics.
- Performance reporting is heterogeneous (different Si sizes, binders, electrolytes, rates), so no cross-study ranking of grafting chemistries is possible from the literature found.

### Gaps
- **Phosphonic acid SAMs on Si/SiOx anodes: no study found** (only on Li metal: *ACS Cent. Sci.* 2018 — [ACS](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acscentsci.8b00845)).
- **Chlorosilane SAMs (e.g., OTS) on Si anodes: no battery study found**; only surface-science OTS on SiNWs — [PMC5146678](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5146678/).
- Alkyne hydrosilylation (Si–C=C), UV/thermal (catalyst-free) hydrosilylation specifically for anodes, and aryl-diazonium monolayers with tailored para-substituents (–CF3, –SO3Li, –PO3H2) were not found.
- Metrics (ICE, retention, SEI thickness) for Jiang 2018, Jiang 2021, Goriparti 2020, Dalla Corte 2016, SAASA 2022, Martin 2010 were not retrievable from snippets.

## Key Question 2 — What is known about whether grafted monolayers survive repeated ~300 % volume expansion and lithiation to Li15Si4?

### Takeaway
No study was found that directly tracks the chemical state of a grafted monolayer (C 1s/F 1s/N 1s XPS, IR) through full lithiation to Li15Si4 and back over many cycles; evidence is indirect (post-cycling impedance, SEI composition "with participation of grafted groups", capacity retention). The best mechanistic baseline comes from Si thin-film model electrodes (NREL SEISta; neutron reflectometry; ATR-FTIR), which show the SEI itself "breathes", LiF forms early and persists, and surface SiOx/silanol consumes Li — but these studies used oxide/H terminations rather than organic SAMs.

### Cited Findings
- The Argonne EO-epoxy monolayer work reports that "surface functional groups suppressed the chemical and electrochemical reactivity of the as-grown and lithiated silicon nanoparticles" and that "a robust Si/electrolyte interphase formed with participation of the grafted organic groups"; XPS shown is for pristine particles — post-lithiation monolayer spectra not located. — [OSTI 1567844](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1567844)
- Dalla Corte 2018: SEI resistance of PMMA-tethered Si films rose far less than bare Si over 26 cycles (indirect evidence of persistent tethered layer). — [Sci. Rep. 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-30000-z)
- Alucone (MLD) coatings on SiNPs are reported to show "excellent flexibility, surviving in the drastic volumetric expansion/shrinkage of the silicon nanoparticle upon lithiation and delithiation", with DFT/AIMD showing the coating lithiates into a dense Li–Al–O ceramic and acts as a "molecular sieve" (Li+ passes, solvent blocked). This is the closest survival evidence, but for a multilayer organic–inorganic film, not a monolayer. — [MD thin-film review, EPJ AP 2026](https://www.epjap.org/articles/epjap/full_html/2026/01/ap20260036/ap20260036.html); [OSTI 1430636](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1430636)
- Si–C-bonded alkyl monolayers are intrinsically robust (HF-stable, thermally stable, slow oxidation), unlike Si–O–C linkages which HF removes — [Sigma-Aldrich technical review](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/technical-documents/technical-article/materials-science-and-engineering/biosensors-and-imaging/molecular-monolayers); but no source connects this to lithiation.
- Model thin-film baselines (no SAM): NREL SEISta prepared a-Si films with low oxygen, native oxide and varying oxide to study SEI; "whether certain surface terminations are most stable" is a stated guiding question; thin films characterized by XPS, SIMS, IR, Raman. — [NREL FY20 Q3](https://www.nrel.gov/media/docs/libraries/tsdc/silicon-electrolyte-interface-stabilization-fy20-q3-progress-report.pdf?sfvrsn=5fc4c105_1); [FY18 Q4](https://www.nrel.gov/media/docs/libraries/tsdc/silicon-electrolyte-interface-stabilization-q4-progress-report-fy18.pdf?sfvrsn=439cbbf9_1)
- 50 nm a-Si: initial capacity loss from both SEI formation and Li reaction with surface SiOx/silanol; LiF forms early and stays permanently while P–O–F/P–F species dissolve; SEI "breathing" (reform/dissolve with SOC). — [OSTI 1785091](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1785091)
- Neutron reflectometry: SEI thickness on Si stays within 160–240 Å rather than growing continuously. — [OSTI 1222554](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1222554)
- Hydrogenated a-Si SEI progression (J. Phys. Chem. C 2012) — [ACS](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jp300787p); combinatorial screening of SEI dynamics on Si — [PMC9543478](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9543478/); operando interface-sensitive XAS of SEI formation — [PMC9568580](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9568580/); FEC gives dense LiF/Li2O near Si — [arXiv 1805.03226](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.03226)
- Chemo-mechanical SEI growth models for Si particles (Kolzenberg et al., *Batteries & Supercaps* 2022; voltage-hysteresis particle–SEI model 2023) treat SEI as a continuum — no monolayer term. — [Wiley](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/batt.202100216); [arXiv 2305.17533](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.17533)
- "Surface Coating Constraint Induced Self-Discharging of Silicon Nanoparticles" (OSTI 1243255) — coatings can induce self-discharge; relevance to SAMs not established. — [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1243255)

### Inferences
- Because Si–C and Si–O–Si anchor bonds are on surface Si atoms that themselves become part of the LixSi alloy (Si–Si bonds break, Li–Si form), monolayer survival is physically doubtful at full lithiation; the literature's silence on post-cycling monolayer spectroscopy suggests this question is largely untested rather than resolved.
- Where benefits persist over hundreds of cycles (PANI-SAM 2000 cycles; 4VP 300 cycles), the likely mechanism is that the monolayer templates the *first* SEI/polymer layer, which then persists — not that the monolayer itself persists.

### Gaps
- No operando (ATR-FTIR/SEIRAS, neutron reflectometry, XPS, nano-FTIR) study of a SAM-modified Si electrode through lithiation was found; Ozanam-group operando IR exists for Si but the SAM-specific paper's content could not be retrieved (search budget exhausted).
- No study on SAM fate upon crystallization to Li15Si4 (<50 mV) vs. capacity-limited cycling was found.
- No TEM/EELS tracking of a monolayer-derived shell on cycled SiNPs was found (available in-situ TEM is for graphene-encapsulated Si — [Sci. Rep. 2014](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep03863)).

## Key Question 3 — Have SAMs been used to covalently anchor binders to Si, and is this scaled beyond lab?

### Takeaway
Yes — covalent SAM→binder coupling has been demonstrated with epoxy-silane/EO-epoxy → PAA (Argonne 2018/2019), amine-SAM → polyimide dianhydride (SiO, full cells, 2020), ATRP-initiator → grafted PAA (2017/2018), and SAM → PANI (2025); patents from battery makers describe aminosilane + FEC double SAMs and silane/PAA slurries. All published demonstrations are coin-cell scale with nano-Si or µm-SiO; no pouch-cell, high-loading (>3 mAh cm⁻²), or calendar-aging data for SAM–binder coupled Si electrodes were found.

### Cited Findings
- Epoxy-SiNPs covalently bond PAA; "much enhanced adhesion strength" of laminate — [ACS AMI 2018](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.8b17729); EO-epoxy monolayer reacted with PAA — [OSTI 1567844](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1567844)
- Amine-SAM on µm-SiO covalently anchors polyimide; full-cell stability verified; reduced electrode swelling — [J. Power Sources 2020](https://www.academia.edu/84376220/Highly_stable_lithium_ion_battery_anode_with_polyimide_coating_anchored_onto_micron_size_silicon_monoxide_via_self_assembled_monolayer); [Small 2024 description](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smll.202403537)
- ATRP-grafted PAA "binder-like molecules covalently linked to SiNPs" enabling self-adhesion, cycled without electrolyte additives — [J. Power Sources 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378775317301453)
- Polydopamine-wrapped Si cross-linked to PAA via amide bonds (amine from dopamine, not silane) — [Univ. Michigan PDF](https://lu.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/347/2017/12/16AMIBie-pw.pdf)
- Binder context: PAA has far more –COOH than CMC/alginate and is most explored for Si — [InfoMat 2021](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/inf2.12185); buffered aqueous conditions promote covalent Si–CMC bonding (ATR-FTIR) — [ResearchGate 2017](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/321031289_A_comparative_study_of_polyacrylic_acid_PAA_and_carboxymethyl_cellulose_CMC_binders_for_Si-based_electrodes); NaPAA-g-CMC — [Sci. Rep. 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep19583); silane-cross-linked PAA binders — [RSC Adv. 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9084488/), [J. Mater. Sci. 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10853-021-06579-6); lysine-cross-linked PAA at 3.03 mg cm⁻² — [ACS AMI 2022](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acsami.2c16997)
- Scale indicators: high-loading SiOx pouch cells (46.3 cm², NMC811) reaching ~1000 cycles were achieved by electrode/electrolyte design *without* SAMs; wrinkling of >5.5 mAh cm⁻² electrodes is the persistent challenge — [arXiv 2509.15144](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.15144); [OSTI 3030692](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/3030692). Si-dominant full cells with binder variation: capacity-limiting the voltage window gives >5× cycle life — [J. Power Sources 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378775325014569). Pouch-cell Si anode test protocols — [IOP 2024](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/1945-7111/ad1630)
- Fang & Liu (*Electrochem* 2021, 2, 664–676, DOI 10.3390/electrochem2040041) caution that "the synthetic complexity of these nano materials and structures could very possibly bring significant challenges in actual large-scale application". — [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-3293/2/4/41)
- Aggregator (Patsnap) pages claim silane loadings of 0.5–3 wt % and peel strengths of 195–225 N/m for Si–O–Cu bonding; **unverified** against primary sources. — [Patsnap](https://eureka.patsnap.com/materials/binder-integrated-silicon-anode)

### Inferences
- Industrial relevance is signalled only by patents (US 11631859 double SAM; US 10991936 silane/PAA slurry; US 11710826 Argonne; Kratos family), not by peer-reviewed pouch-cell data.
- SAM–binder coupling appears to be pursued mainly to raise adhesion/peel strength and limit electrode swelling, which is exactly the metric that matters at high loading — yet it has not been tested there.

### Gaps
- No study of SAM-coupled binder with alginate or CMC on Si via aminosilane/EDC-NHS was found (query could not be run).
- No peel-strength or electrode-swelling data at ≥3 mAh cm⁻² for SAM-primed Si electrodes; no dry-process (PTFE) compatibility studies; no slurry-stability (silane hydrolysis in aqueous slurries) studies found.
- Assignee of US 11,631,859 not retrieved.

## Key Question 4 — What computational work exists on molecular monolayers on (lithiated) Si surfaces?

### Takeaway
Extensive first-principles/AIMD/ReaxFF work exists on bare Si or oxide/alucone-coated Si–electrolyte interfaces, and the first machine-learned-force-field studies of Li transport at Si interfaces are appearing, but **no DFT, MD or MLIP study of an organic SAM (silane, alkyl, aryl, phosphonate) on a lithiated Si surface in contact with electrolyte was found.**

### Cited Findings
- Alucone-coated lithiated Si: DFT + AIMD of EC reduction at film/electrolyte interface; coating lithiates to dense Li–Al–O; "molecular sieve" behaviour. — [OSTI 1430636](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1430636); [EPJ AP 2026 review](https://www.epjap.org/articles/epjap/full_html/2026/01/ap20260036/ap20260036.html)
- AIMD of FEC decomposition on Si with/without thin Li4SiO4 layer — [arXiv 1401.4165](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.4165)
- Chemical-potential-controlled ReaxFF MD of Si–electrolyte interface under multicycle lithiation (surface roughening, Si dissolution) with DFT of EC ring-opening on Si(111), *ACS AMI* 2026 — [ACS](https://pubs.acs.org/aamick/article/18/9/13732/5077113/Dynamic-Evolution-and-Degradation-of-Silicon); [PMC12983198](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12983198/)
- ReaxFF of Si-core/Al2O3- and SiO2-shell: softer-outer/harder-inner modulus gradient gives mechanically stable coating — [OSTI 1387538](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1387538); ReaxFF SiNW lithiation stress retards reaction front — [OSTI 1265270](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1265270)
- DFT lithiation of amorphous SiO2 and Si (Sivonxay, Persson 2019) — [LBL PDF](https://perssongroup.lbl.gov/papers/Sivonxay-2019-Li.pdf); review of first-principles anode/electrolyte interface simulations — [UT Austin PDF](https://utw10370.utweb.utexas.edu/publications/P167.pdf); first-principles lithiation of Si (2013) — [arXiv 1303.0933](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.0933); crystalline vs amorphous Si MD — [PMC11818356](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11818356/)
- Multiscale SEI modelling reviews — [arXiv 1811.04667](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.04667); [npj Comput. Mater. 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-018-0064-0)
- Machine-learned force fields + Markov state models for Li transport (2025) — not SAM-specific — [arXiv 2511.20863](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.20863)
- Electronic background: carriers cross Si-bound alkyl monolayers by tunneling — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7291538_How_Do_Electronic_Carriers_Cross_Si-Bound_Alkyl_Monolayers); dipole effects of grafted alkylamine monolayers on Si/SiO2 — [arXiv 1810.00805](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.00805)

### Inferences
- The alucone work supplies a template (AIMD of electrolyte reduction through a lithiating organic–inorganic film) that could be adapted directly to silane/alkyl SAMs, but nobody appears to have done it.
- ReaxFF parameterizations exist for Si/Li/O/C/H and for Si/Al/O, so a SAM-on-Si lithiation ReaxFF or MLIP study is technically feasible with existing tools.

### Gaps
- Targeted query "first-principles DFT functionalized silicon surface organic monolayer lithium adsorption anode" could not be run (budget exhausted); conclusion of "no work found" rests on the ~8 computational queries that did run.

## Key Question 5 — Which open questions do reviews identify (and which does this survey add)?

### Takeaway
Reviews (Fang & Liu 2021; He et al. 2024; the 2026 "interface-adaptive Si anode" review; Fedorov 2021; electrolyte reviews 2025–2026) identify scale-up complexity, the need for self-healing/dynamic covalent interphases, covalent anchoring via Si–O–Si/Si–N, and the lack of LiF-rich mechanically robust SEI as open issues. Combining these with the study-by-study survey above yields a concrete list of unexplored directions (below).

### Cited Findings
- Fang & Liu, *Electrochem* 2021: surveys MOFs, COFs and monolayers on Si; "Si electrochemistry has strong dependence on the property of the Si interface"; bottom-up molecular structures have "well-defined molecular structures and thus 2D or 3D nano-ordered configurations"; caveat on synthetic complexity for large-scale application. — [MDPI](https://doi.org/10.3390/electrochem2040041)
- "Toward interface-adaptive silicon anodes: From 3D SEI to dynamic coupled interphases" (2026 review): argues for dynamic covalent chemistry and supramolecular motifs so the interphase self-heals as particles breathe; identifies polymers with silanol or aminosilane groups anchoring the SEI covalently through Si–O–Si or Si–N bonds via condensation with surface hydroxyls. — [PMC13457186](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13457186/)
- He et al., *Small* 2024 (SAMs in rechargeable batteries): reviews SAMs across electrodes incl. the SiO/SAM/polyimide system; only quantitative Si-related figure in snippet is for a different Si–Gr full cell (83.1 % vs 58.2 % retention over 300 cycles at 1C). — [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smll.202403537)
- Fedorov et al., "Molecular Engineering Approaches to Fabricate Artificial SEIs on Anodes for Li-Ion Batteries: A Critical Review", *Adv. Energy Mater.* 2021, DOI 10.1002/aenm.202101173 — contents not retrievable. — [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aenm.202101173)
- Liquid-electrolyte review for Si (2025): free-solvent-dominated solvation gives thick porous SEI that cannot accommodate volume change; fluorinated/LiF-rich SEI more robust. — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44405-025-00004-1); Si SEI is three-layered with lithium silicate/silica near Si — [PMC12300681](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12300681/); electrolyte engineering review 2026 (–CF3 groups give F-rich organic outer SEI; –OCH2CH3 of VTEO forms Si–O–Li) — [OAE Publishing](https://www.oaepublish.com/articles/energyz.2026.17); comprehensive Si anode review 2024 — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949821X24000814); ALD/MLD on Si review — [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1137443)
- Calendar aging: FEC in-situ polymerization boosts Si calendar life via surface passivation (2025 preprint) — no SAM calendar-aging study found. — [arXiv 2512.01084](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.01084)
- Prelithiation: role of prelithiation in Si interface stabilization — [arXiv 2304.06817](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.06817); pre-constructed adaptive SEI on chemically prelithiated SiO — [PMC12752609](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12752609/); electrochemical prelithiation of mesoporous Si films raised full-cell ICE 58.9→85.3 % — [IOP 2025](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/1945-7111/adf5ee). No SAM + prelithiation combination found.
- SiOx vs Si surface: deliberate surface oxidation of nano-Si as cycle-life method — [PMC7570913](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7570913/); surface chemistry of SiNPs in Si/Ni3.4Sn4 composites — [PMC7823592](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7823592/). No paper compares the same SAM on SiOx vs oxide-free Si.

### Inferences — consolidated gap list (for the report)
1. **Untested molecule classes on Si anodes:** phosphonic acids; chlorosilanes (OTS, FOTS/FDTS perfluoroalkyl); alkyne hydrosilylation; aryl-diazonium with ionic (–SO3Li, –CO2Li, –PO3Li2) or –CF3 para groups as LiF/Li-salt templates; mixed/binary SAMs; zwitterionic or Li-single-ion-conducting monolayers; redox-active or dynamic-covalent (boronate ester, imine, disulfide) self-healing monolayers.
2. **Commercially relevant conditions:** no SAM–Si data at >3 mAh cm⁻², Si-dominant (>50 %) electrodes, pouch cells, lean electrolyte, 45 °C, or calendar aging; only two full-cell reports (SiO/SAM/PI; EC-grafted SiNPs patent).
3. **SAM fate under 300 % expansion / Li15Si4:** no operando or post-mortem spectroscopy of the grafted layer itself; no comparison of capacity-limited vs full lithiation on monolayer retention.
4. **SAM–binder covalent coupling:** demonstrated with PAA and polyimide only; alginate/CMC/PVDF-free dry processing untested; no high-loading adhesion metrics; silane hydrolysis stability in aqueous slurry unaddressed.
5. **SAM + prelithiation:** no study of SAM compatibility with Li-metal contact, Li-arene chemical prelithiation, or LixSi stabilization — a notable gap because both SAMs and prelithiation target ICE.
6. **SiOx vs Si:** no controlled comparison of alkoxysilane (oxide-anchored) vs hydrosilylation (Si–C) chemistry on the same material; most commercial materials are SiOx or carbon-coated Si–C where the outer surface is carbon — SAM chemistry for the carbon shell of Si–C composites (diazonium) is barely explored.
7. **Modeling:** zero DFT/AIMD/ReaxFF/MLIP work on organic monolayers on lithiating Si; tunneling-limit (2.5 nm) observation from NREL has no theoretical treatment.
8. **Operando characterization:** no operando IR/NR/XPS/EQCM/AFM of SAM-modified Si; thin-film model-electrode programs (NREL SEISta) have varied oxide but not organic SAMs.
9. **Design rule conflict:** passivating SAMs raise ICE but dense hydrophobic SAMs electronically isolate Si — the trade-off between electronic tunneling, Li+ permeability and SEI templating has not been mapped as a function of chain length/head group.

### Gaps
- Contents of the Fedorov 2021 AEM critical review and the He 2024 *Small* review (their explicit open-question lists) were not retrievable; the gap list above is therefore primarily this survey's inference from primary-study coverage, not a quotation of those reviews.
- Several candidate primary papers from memory (e.g., Ozanam/Chazalviel-group operando ATR-FTIR on grafted Si; methyl-terminated Si(111) in battery electrolyte; Buriak/Veinot alkyl-Si nanocrystal anodes) could not be confirmed with URLs and are **not** asserted here.
